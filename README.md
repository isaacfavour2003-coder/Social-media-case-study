


# 30-Day Social Media Content Plan for a Small Business

A sample content plan I created to show how a small business can plan a full month of social media posts.

**Example business:** Sweet Crumbs Bakery (a fictional home bakery selling cakes, cupcakes and small-chops)

**Platforms:** Instagram (main), WhatsApp Status, TikTok

**Prepared by:** Isaac Favour Chimdinma

> This is a practice project using a made-up business. It shows my planning process and the way I think about content.

---

## 1. Goals

- Get more people to know the brand
- Turn followers into customers (orders through DM or WhatsApp)
- Post consistently, 5 days a week

## 2. Target audience

- Young adults and families who order cakes for birthdays and events
- Busy people who want quick, tasty snacks delivered
- People who search for "cakes near me" on social media

## 3. Content pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Share of posts |
|---|---|---|
| Showcase | Show products and finished work | 30% |
| Educate | Tips, how-tos and baking facts | 25% |
| Behind the scenes | Build trust and personality | 20% |
| Social proof | Customer reviews and orders | 15% |
| Promotion | Offers and calls to action | 10% |

## 4. Weekly posting rhythm

| Day | Theme |
|---|---|
| Monday | Tip of the week |
| Tuesday | Product showcase |
| Wednesday | Behind the scenes |
| Thursday | Customer love |
| Friday | Offer or order reminder |
| Weekend | Rest, reply to comments and DMs |

## 5. The 30-day plan

### Week 1: Introduce the brand
| Day | Post idea | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome post: who we are and what we bake | Carousel |
| 2 | Our best-selling cake, close-up photo | Photo |
| 3 | A day in the kitchen | Short video |
| 4 | Meet the baker | Photo and caption |
| 5 | "How to order" guide | Carousel |
| 6 | Poll: favourite cake flavour | Story |
| 7 | Weekend rest | N/A |

### Week 2: Build trust
| Day | Post idea | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 3 tips for storing a cake | Carousel |
| 9 | Cupcake showcase | Photo |

| 11 | First customer review | Graphic |
| 12 | Friday offer: free delivery on orders over a set amount | Graphic |

| 14 | Weekend rest | N/A |

| Day | Post idea | Format |

| 17 | Packaging process | Short video |
| 18 | Customer photo shout-out | Photo |
| 19 | Order reminder for upcoming events | Graphic |

| 21 | Weekend rest | N/A |

### Week 4: Convert followers to customers
| Day | Post idea | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 22 | How to choose a cake size for your event | Carousel |
| 23 | Custom cake design showcase | Photo |
| 24 | Decorating time-lapse | Short video |

### Days 29-30: Review
| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 29 | Check what worked: likes, comments, saves, shares, profile visits |
| 30 | Plan next month, based on the best-performing posts |

## 6. Sample captions

**Product showcase:**
> Fresh, soft and made with love. Our vanilla cupcakes are ready for your next celebration. DM to order today!

**Educational tip:**
> Tip: keep your cake in a cool place, away from direct sunlight, so the icing stays smooth. Save this for later!

**Offer:**
> This Friday only: free delivery on orders over [amount]. Send us a DM to place yours.

## 7. How I would measure success

- **Reach and views:** how many people see each post
- **Engagement:** likes, comments, shares and saves
- **Profile visits** and **new followers**
- **Orders** that come through DMs or WhatsApp

At the end of the month, I would compare these numbers with the first week and repeat the content types that perform best.

## 8. Tools I would use

- **Canva:** post and carousel designs
- **CapCut:** short video editing
- **Google Sheets:** content calendar
- **Instagram Insights:** tracking results

## 9. What I learned

- Planning a full month ahead makes posting consistent and less stressful.
- Mixing education, trust-building and promotion works better than posting only offers.
- Tracking results is what turns a plan into a strategy.
